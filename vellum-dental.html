Your first visit

What actually happens, step by step

Most new patients want to know this before they book. Here's the honest sequence.

Consultation and X-ray

A 15-minute conversation about what's bothering you, followed by a digital X-ray if needed — results shown to you on screen, not just described.

Treatment plan, with costs upfront

You'll get a written plan with every procedure and its cost before anything begins. No mid-treatment surprises.

Treatment

Procedures are scheduled at your pace — nothing is bundled into one long, uncomfortable sitting unless you prefer it that way.

Follow-up check

A free follow-up within two weeks for anything beyond a routine cleaning, to make sure everything has settled properly.
