Three generations, one filter.

What started as a single steel filter on a pushcart is now a corner everyone in the neighbourhood knows.

Kaapi Junction began in 1987, when Rajagopal started serving coffee to bus conductors and mill workers outside T. Nagar station. The decoction recipe hasn't changed since — the same ratio of chicory, the same eighteen-hour brew.

Today his grandson runs the counter, but the steel filters are the same ones his grandfather bought secondhand in his first year. Regulars still ask for their coffee "extra strong, less water," and the staff knows exactly what that means.
